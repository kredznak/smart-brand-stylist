# Privacy policy for Smart Brand Stylist

**Last updated: 21 September 2026**

Smart Brand Stylist is an add-on for Adobe Express that turns a logo or a website into a brand kit, suggests copy, and finds off-brand colors and fonts in your design.

This policy explains exactly what the add-on does with your information. The short version: there are no accounts, no tracking, and nothing you give it is kept. Most of the work happens on your own computer.

## What the add-on does with your information

### Your logo

When you upload a logo, **the colors are read on your own computer**. The image is not sent anywhere for this. It never leaves your device.

There is one exception, and it only happens if you ask for it. If you press **"Match fonts to my logo (AI)"**, the logo image is sent to our server and on to Anthropic, so that Claude can look at it and suggest typefaces that suit it. The button tells you this before you press it. If you never press it, your logo is never uploaded.

### Your brand details

When you ask for copy suggestions, the brand name, description and tone of voice you typed are sent to our server and on to Anthropic, so that Claude can write the suggestions. That is the only thing they are used for.

### A website address

If you enter your website instead of a logo, the address is sent to our server. The server visits that public page and reads its colors and font names, then sends those back to the add-on. The page is not sent to Anthropic, and no AI is involved.

The server only accepts public web addresses. Addresses on a private or local network are refused.

### Your brand kit

Your saved palette, fonts, logo thumbnail and brand voice are stored **by Adobe Express on your own device**. They are not sent to us and we cannot see them. Removing the add-on removes them.

### Technical information

When the add-on contacts our server, the server sees your IP address, as any web server does. It is used for one purpose: counting requests so that nobody can run up a large bill. The count is stored as a number against your IP address and **is deleted automatically after 48 hours**. It is not used to identify you, is not linked to anything else, and is not used to build a profile.

Our server is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep its own short-term request logs as part of running the service.

## What we do not do

- We do not create accounts or ask who you are.
- We do not store the content of your requests. Your brand details, logo and website address are passed straight through to generate a result and are not written to any database or log by us.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies.
- We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
- We do not use anything you provide to train AI models.

## Who else is involved

**Anthropic** provides Claude, the AI that writes the copy suggestions and matches fonts to your logo. When you use those two features, your brand details or logo are processed by Anthropic to produce the result. Anthropic's privacy policy is at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy, and their commercial terms state that inputs submitted through their API are not used to train their models.

**Cloudflare** hosts the small server described above. Their privacy policy is at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

**Adobe** provides Express itself, and stores your brand kit on your device through its add-on storage. Adobe's privacy policy is at https://www.adobe.com/privacy/policy.html.

## Your choices

- The colors-from-a-logo and website features work without any AI. If you never press the AI buttons, nothing is sent to Anthropic.
- To delete your brand kit, remove the add-on from Adobe Express, or start a new brand kit to replace it.
- Because we do not hold any information about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete if you ask. The counter tied to your IP address disappears on its own within 48 hours.

## Children

This add-on is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this add-on starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change.
